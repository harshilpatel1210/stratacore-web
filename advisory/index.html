Standardized Workflow

The 3-Stage Delivery Cadence

Every modeling engagement follows a disciplined 3-stage delivery roadmap to ensure formula integrity, client transparency, and audit readiness.

Stage 1: Ingestion & Driver Audit

Historical book intake (24–36 months), chart of accounts cleaning, non-recurring adjustments, and driver baseline verification.

Stage 2: Structural Logic Sign-Off

Delivery of preliminary financial models (or valuation draft) for management assumption review and operational logic validation.

Stage 3: Unlocked Hand-off & Debrief

Final release of editable, formula-visible Excel workbooks, dynamic scenario toggles, executive slide decks, and live walkthroughs.
