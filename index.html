Advisory Fundamentals

The Anatomy of Three-Statement Financial Modeling

A single, unlinked forecast sheet leaves management blind to cash flow timing. Institutional financial modeling integrates all three statements into a dynamic mathematical engine.

1. Dynamic Income Statement

Projects revenue trajectories, COGS, and operational overhead. EBITDA is dynamically linked to taxation, interest schedules, and retained earnings.

2. Balance Sheet Mechanics

Tracks assets, liabilities, and equity. Working capital drivers (DSO, DIO, DPO), debt amortization schedules, and capital asset depreciation balance automatically.

3. Cash Flow Statement Linkage

Reconciles operational cash with balance sheet swings, Capex requirements, and financing cash flows to reveal the true net monthly burn rate and runway date.

Valuation Mechanics

Corporate Valuation & Capital Budgeting Principles

How institutional investors and corporate boards quantitatively evaluate enterprise value and allocate capital across growth opportunities.

Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)

Discounts projected Unlevered Free Cash Flows (UFCF) back to present value using a derived hurdle rate, isolating operating value from capital structure.

Cost of Capital (WACC)

Calculates the blended cost of debt and equity capital using the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), establishing the minimum hurdle rate an investment must clear.

Capital Budgeting (NPV & IRR)

Provides objective decision criteria for corporate asset acquisitions and expansions by calculating Net Present Value (NPV) and Internal Rate of Return (IRR).
